Durations are h:mm:ss. Per-substrate time = scans × time per scan + switch-over, plus the margin. The setup comes first and the powder and equipment changes are placed between groups, without a margin. "Fits" is cumulative in table order: "Fits" means the group completes with its safety margin intact, "Fits inside the safety margin" that it completes only by using that margin. One timeline row is one 24 h day; lighter shades are each group's margin, hatched grey the breaks, and the red line the end of the beamtime.
